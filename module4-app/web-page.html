The problemPage 2

There's a half-painted dresser in the garage, two of the same glue gun in the drawer, and you haven't made anything in a month.

Big projects stall because nothing keeps track of what you own, what you need, what it costs, and how long the paint takes to dry. And on busy weeks, there's no small thing to make instead, so you make nothing.

You didn't run out of creativity. You ran out of a plan, and a place to start.

FeaturesPage 3

Infinite creative muses, one app.

My Projects

Your projects, with a real plan.

Put in anything you're making. Artistic Intelligence checks your supplies, adds up the cost, and builds a schedule that fits your deadline.

  • Supply shelf: know what you have before you buy.
  • Step timeline: honest time estimates, drying time included.
  • Budget view: totals and cheaper swaps.
  • Priority ranking: keep the project with the deadline on track.

Daily Spark

Something to make, every day.

A new creative prompt every day. You pick how much time you've got.

  • Sketch: 5 minutes. Draw your coffee mug with your other hand.
  • Study: 30 minutes. Make a bookmark from an old magazine page.
  • Masterpiece: a weekend. Turn a thrifted frame into a mosaic mirror.

After 30 days in a row, it asks if you're ready for harder ones. You decide.
