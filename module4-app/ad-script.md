# Ad Script: Artistic Intelligence (30-second video)

**Format:** vertical video (social) · **Length:** ~30 seconds · **Tone:** plays on the "AI" name, relatable, upbeat finish

| Time | Visual | Audio / Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:04 | Dark screen, glowing "AI" text with a robotic hum, like a tech ad. | **VO (serious, techy):** "Introducing… AI." |
| 0:04–0:07 | Record-scratch. The glowing text gets splattered with paint. Big hand-lettered text fills the screen: **NOT THAT KIND OF AI.** Then it resolves into **Artistic Intelligence**. | **VO (normal, warm, a little smug):** "Not that kind of AI." |
| 0:07–0:11 | Quick cuts: a half-painted chair, a crochet project with the needle still in it, a drawer with two identical glue guns. | **VO:** "Half-finished projects. Supplies you bought twice." |
| 0:11–0:14 | Maker on the couch scrolling their phone, sketchbook closed beside them. | **VO:** "And weeks where you don't make anything at all." |
| 0:14–0:20 | Screen recording: My Projects shows the chair project with "Have ✓ / Need to buy," a cost total, and "Paint dries: 4 hrs" on the timeline. | **VO:** "Artistic Intelligence plans your projects: what you have, what you need, and how long it'll really take." |
| 0:20–0:25 | Screen: Daily Spark with three cards: Sketch, Study, Masterpiece. Maker taps Sketch and draws their mug with their left hand, laughing. | **VO:** "And every day, it gives you something small to make. Five minutes or a whole weekend. You pick." |
| 0:25–0:30 | Finished chair, a wall of little sketches, streak counter ticks up. End card: **Artistic Intelligence. Make something today.** | **VO:** "Artistic Intelligence. The only AI that makes you the artist." *(music hit)* |

## Notes

- **Music:** starts as a cold synth hum (0:00–0:04), then switches to light, upbeat acoustic at the record-scratch.
- **Attention getter:** "Not that kind of AI." is the hook. It has to land in the first 7 seconds, before people scroll. Make it the biggest text in the video, and use it as the thumbnail and the caption when posting.
- **On-screen text:** caption every line for viewers watching on mute.
- **Call to action:** end card with "Pay once. Free with a .edu email." and the app link.
- **Alternate cut:** swap the 0:14–0:20 shot for a maker sharing a TikTok craft post to the app and watching it turn into a step-by-step task.
- **Claims:** don't add statistics to the ad unless they're sourced in `evidence.md`.
