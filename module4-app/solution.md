# Solution: Artistic Intelligence (AI)

**Artistic Intelligence**, or **AI**, is a planner for craft and DIY projects that also gives you a small creative task every day. You put in your own projects and it helps you finish them. On days you don't have time for those, it gives you something small to make anyway.

## Part 1: My Projects

Add any personal project: a painted dresser, a quilt, a costume, a birdhouse, a jacket upcycle.

| Problem (from `problem.md`) | Feature |
| --- | --- |
| No clear supply list | **Supply shelf.** Log the supplies you own once. When you add a project, AI splits its list into *have* and *need to buy*. |
| Time is a guess | **Step timeline.** Breaks the project into steps with low–high time ranges, including waiting time for paint, glue, stain, and dye. |
| Costs creep | **Budget view.** Totals the shopping list and suggests cheaper swaps, like thrifted or leftover materials. |
| Too many projects at once | **Priority ranking.** Rank your projects and set deadlines. AI builds a schedule around your busy days. |

## Part 2: Daily Spark

Every day, AI suggests a small creative task. You pick the level that fits your day:

| Level | Time | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sketch** | ~5 minutes | Draw your coffee mug with your other hand. |
| **Study** | ~30 minutes | Make a bookmark from a page of an old magazine. |
| **Masterpiece** | A weekend or longer | Turn a thrifted frame into a mirror with a mosaic border. |

| Problem (from `problem.md`) | Feature |
| --- | --- |
| Blank-page freeze | **A prompt is always waiting.** No deciding what to make; just start. |
| All-or-nothing thinking | **Sketch-level tasks** take five minutes, so there's always time to make something. |
| Wrong difficulty | **Three levels every day.** As you finish tasks, AI suggests harder ones in the crafts you like and keeps easy ones for busy days. |

Daily Spark also uses what you have. Prompts can be built from supplies already on your supply shelf, so you don't need to buy anything to start.

## How the two parts connect

- If you have a project in progress, Daily Spark can suggest a step from it as today's task ("Sand one drawer, 20 minutes").
- If a Masterpiece prompt grows into something you love, save it to My Projects with one tap.
- A streak calendar shows every day you made something, big or small.

## How it works

1. **Set up:** pick the crafts you like and log what's in your supply bin.
2. **Add a project:** describe it, paste a tutorial link, or upload a pattern.
3. **Get a plan:** shopping list with costs, step-by-step schedule, deadline check.
4. **Open the app each day:** pick Sketch, Study, or Masterpiece, or work on a project step.
5. **Log it:** take a photo, mark it done, and watch your gallery and streak grow.

## Who it's for first

Students and busy hobbyists who want to keep making things in small pieces of time.

## What success looks like

- More personal projects finished.
- Fewer duplicate supply purchases.
- More days with some kind of creative activity.

## Limits and next steps

- Time estimates start as general ranges and get better as users log real times.
- The prompt library needs to be written and sorted by craft and level before launch.
- Next step: a clickable prototype of the Daily Spark screen and the "add project → plan" flow to test with the people surveyed in `evidence.md`.
