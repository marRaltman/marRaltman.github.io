# Solution: Artistic Intelligence (AI)

**Artistic Intelligence**, or **AI**, is a planner for craft and DIY projects that also gives you a small creative task every day. You put in your own projects and it helps you finish them. On days you don't have time for those, it gives you something small to make anyway.

It works like a **private notebook with a shared task library**. There's no feed, no algorithm deciding what you see, no promoted posts, and no paid or sponsored accounts.

## Part 1: My Projects

Add any personal project: a painted dresser, a quilt, a costume, a birdhouse, a jacket upcycle.

| Problem (from `problem.md`) | Feature |
| --- | --- |
| No clear supply list | **Supply shelf.** Log the supplies you own once. When you add a project, AI splits its list into *have* and *need to buy*. |
| Time is a guess | **Step timeline.** Breaks the project into steps with low–high time ranges, including waiting time for paint, glue, stain, and dye. |
| Costs creep | **Budget view.** Totals the shopping list and suggests cheaper swaps, like thrifted or leftover materials. |
| Too many projects at once | **Priority ranking.** Rank your projects and set deadlines. AI builds a schedule around your busy days. |

## Part 2: Daily Spark

Every day, AI suggests a small creative task. You pick the level that fits your day:

| Level | Time | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sketch** | ~5 minutes | Draw your coffee mug with your other hand. |
| **Study** | ~30 minutes | Make a bookmark from a page of an old magazine. |
| **Masterpiece** | A weekend or longer | Turn a thrifted frame into a mirror with a mosaic border. |

| Problem (from `problem.md`) | Feature |
| --- | --- |
| Blank-page freeze | **A prompt is always waiting.** No deciding what to make; just start. |
| All-or-nothing thinking | **Sketch-level tasks** take five minutes, so there's always time to make something. |
| Wrong difficulty | **Three levels every day,** plus a level-up check-in (below) when you're ready for more. |

Daily Spark also uses what you have. Prompts can be built from supplies already on your supply shelf, so you don't need to buy anything to start.

### Where the tasks come from

1. **The starter library.** A pre-written list of tasks that ships with the app, sorted by craft and level.
2. **Shared tasks.** Tasks other users have chosen to add to the shared library.
3. **Imported posts.** See a tutorial or craft on social media (TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest)? Share the post to AI. The app reads the post, pulls out the supply list and steps, and turns them into an organized task. This is modeled on the recipe app **Osta** (https://ostarecipes.com/welcome), which imports recipes from Instagram, TikTok, and websites and turns them into organized recipes. Artistic Intelligence does the same for crafts: supplies instead of ingredients, steps instead of instructions. This is the one place the app uses actual artificial intelligence: to read the post. You're still the one making the thing.

When you add or import a task:
- **You choose private or shared.** Private tasks stay in your own notebook. Shared tasks go into the library for everyone.
- **The app suggests a level** (Sketch, Study, or Masterpiece) from the time and number of steps, and you can change it.

### Leveling up

The app never makes your tasks harder on its own. After about **30 days in a row** of doing at least one task a day (any level), it asks if you want to take it to the next level. You say yes or "not yet." If you say not yet, it asks again after another 30 days.

## How the two parts connect

- If you have a project in progress, Daily Spark can suggest a step from it as today's task ("Sand one drawer, 20 minutes").
- If a Masterpiece prompt grows into something you love, save it to My Projects with one tap.
- A streak calendar shows every day you made something, big or small.

## Your notebook stays private

- Your gallery, projects, and supply shelf are private.
- Other people only see tasks you choose to share, not your photos or progress.
- No following, likes, comments, feed, ads, or promoted posts.

## Price and accounts

- **One-time purchase.** Pay once, no subscription.
- **Free for students and educators** with a valid `.edu` email.
- **Main and backup email.** When you sign up, the app asks for a main email and a backup email. If your `.edu` email stops working (for example, after you graduate), you sign in with your backup email and keep your account, your notebook, and your free access.
- An account made with a `.edu` email never has to pay later. You'd only pay if you start a brand-new account with a non-`.edu` email.

## How it works

1. **Set up:** make an account with a main and backup email, pick the crafts you like, and log what's in your supply bin.
2. **Add a project:** describe it, paste a tutorial link, upload a pattern, or share a social media post to the app.
3. **Get a plan:** shopping list with costs, step-by-step schedule, deadline check.
4. **Open the app each day:** pick Sketch, Study, or Masterpiece, or work on a project step.
5. **Log it:** take a photo, mark it done, and watch your private gallery and streak grow.

## Who it's for first

Students and busy hobbyists who want to keep making things in small pieces of time. Free `.edu` access makes students the easiest group to start with.

## What success looks like

- More personal projects finished.
- Fewer duplicate supply purchases.
- More days with some kind of creative activity.

## Limits and next steps

- Time estimates start as general ranges and get better as users log real times.
- The starter library needs to be written and sorted by craft and level before launch.
- Post import depends on what each social platform allows apps to read, and won't work on every post. Osta's App Store reviews mention TikTok imports being less reliable than Instagram, so the app should always let users fix an import by hand.
- Shared tasks need a way to report broken or unsafe tasks.
- Next step: a clickable prototype of the Daily Spark screen, post import, and the "add project → plan" flow to test with the people surveyed in `evidence.md`.
