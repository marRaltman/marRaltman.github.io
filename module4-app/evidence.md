## 1. Making art is good for people

- In a study of 39 adults, **45 minutes of art making** lowered levels of cortisol (a stress hormone) for about **75%** of participants, and prior art experience didn't matter.
  - Source: Kaimal, G., Ray, K., & Muniz, J. (2016). "Reduction of Cortisol Levels and Participants' Responses Following Art Making." *Art Therapy*, 33(2), 74–80.
- In a daily-diary study of 658 young adults, people who did something creative on a given day reported more **positive mood and "flourishing"** the next day.
  - Source: Conner, T. S., DeYoung, C. G., & Silvia, P. J. (2018). "Everyday creative activity as a path to flourishing." *The Journal of Positive Psychology*, 13(2), 181–189.

**What this shows:** Even small amounts of daily creativity help, and you don't need to be experienced. This supports the daily-task half of the app.
